## What these tools usually are

Most results for this kind of search are browser tools, not one official app. You open the site, confirm age, pick a ready character or build one, and keep the conversation in a single thread. Some of those tools also have an app. Many do not. The browser version is the one that works without an install.

The chat is the part most people start with. You set a name, a look and a short background if you build. You send messages. You can leave and return. Earlier details are more likely to come back on paid plans. Free tiers forget sooner. Pictures of the character and voice replies usually unlock on paid plans. Most sites ask you to confirm age before you continue.

These are separate sites. They are not ChatGPT itself. They use similar chat models and add a character layer, an image generator and sometimes voice. The product is built around companions, not general assistance.

## How the flow usually works

1. Open the site and confirm age.  
2. Pick a ready character or open the builder.  
3. Set a name, a look and a short background if you build.  
4. Send a few messages and see if the tone fits.  
5. Stay in that thread if you want continuity.  
6. Add the site to your home screen if you want an icon.  
7. Upgrade if the free messages run out and you want to continue.

Most people try one ready character first. If the replies feel right, they stay or build another. If not, they close the tab. The free tier is a test of tone, not a long session of unlimited chat or images.

## Free tier vs paid

| Feature | Free | Paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messages | Few | Higher or unlimited |
| Characters | Limited | More |
| Image generation | Limited or none | Available |
| Voice replies | Limited or none | Available |
| Memory in thread | Shorter | Better |
| Home screen icon | Available | Available |

The free tier ends quickly on purpose. A handful of messages is common. Image generation is usually metered. Chips or similar units run out. Paid plans raise the message limit and unlock the image and voice tools. Memory improves on paid. The renewal price is the number that matters. First-month discounts are common. Yearly billing is often cheaper per month.

## What to check before you pay

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Free message count | A handful is not a daily allowance |
| What paid unlocks | More chat, images, voice, or just higher limits |
| Image meter | How many pictures the plan includes |
| Renewal price | First month is often discounted |
| Cancel path | Account page or support ticket |
| Personal data | Do not put real names or numbers in the chat |
| App vs browser | Many tools have no store app |

A couple of days of free use usually shows if the character holds the conversation. After that the choice is about media and how long you want to stay in one thread. Open the plan page and read the current numbers before you enter a card.
